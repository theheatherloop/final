Relax, Explore, Enjoy

Nestled in a beautiful valley in the Swiss Alps, our resort is the perfect escape from the mundane. Dining options include an Italian restaurant, and ski-in bars on mountainside terraces. Other amenities consist of an upscale spa, hot tubs, a gym, and a climbing wall. Breakfast is available each morning.

Amenities and Options for Everyone

Escape from everyday life and spend a few warm and relaxing days with us. Enjoy the refreshing mountain air. Discover the village and surrounding mountains on foot or by bike. Visit our spa or ski runs during winter, or take a guided hike in the warmer months.

An Escape For Any Time of Year

Whether you prefer a snowy mountain adventure or a peaceful summer getaway, we have it all. Build a snowman on winter slopes, or go for hikes, picnics, and lake swims in the spring and summer.
